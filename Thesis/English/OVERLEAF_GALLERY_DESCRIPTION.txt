This LaTeX template is an English-language adaptation of the official Faculty of Data Science undergraduate graduation thesis/report format at City University of Macau, prepared for the Bachelor of Computer Science programme.

It follows the faculty's official requirements for a unified thesis/report cover, approval page, front matter, chapter structure, references, appendix, and thesis/report formatting.

Official Faculty guideline page:
https://fds.cityu.edu.mo/upcoming_events/552

Supporting English Faculty page with undergraduate graduation project thesis/report requirements and related annexes:
https://fds.cityu.edu.mo/en/upcoming_events/309

This project is not maintained by City University of Macau. It is an unofficial LaTeX implementation based on the official Faculty of Data Science guidelines and template documents.
